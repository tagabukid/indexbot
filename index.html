Risk notice: this is not financial advice and past whale performance does not predict future results. Copying a public trade means you are always slightly behind the whale, and other, faster bots may already be reacting to the same trades. Only connect a wallet funded with what you can afford to lose, and re-verify all contract addresses on robinhoodchain.blockscout.com yourself before enabling LIVE mode.
